Agreement $contract_id, issued $issued_utc (UTC).

1. Parties
   $firm_name ("the Firm"), and
   $trader_name, customer $cif, identity verified under case $kyc_case
   ("the Trader").

2. The account
   Account $acc, product $product_name, allocation $allocation_usd (USD).
   Rule set $rsv_id: maximum loss $max_loss_percent % ($max_loss_kind),
   daily loss limit $daily_loss_percent %, consistency rule $consistency.
   The account's rules are those of rule set $rsv_id; they do not change
   while the account is open.

3. Nature of the account
   $disclosure

4. Profit split and payouts
   The Trader receives $split_percent % of the profit made on the account
   and payable under these terms$split_ladder. The first payout may be requested from
   the trading day $first_after_days days after that of the first trade on
   the account (trading days end at 17:00 New York time), and each later
   one $interval_days days after the previous payout; the minimum payout is
   $minimum_usd (USD). Every payout needs the approval of $approvals people
   at the Firm; once approved, it is taken from the account and sent to the
   Trader's own wallet, whose address the Trader gives and is responsible
   for. A breach of the account's rules ends the account; profit is never
   paid twice.

5. Scaling
   After $review_months months, with at least $min_net_profit_percent % net
   profit and $min_paid_payouts paid payouts, the allocation may grow by
   $scaling_step_percent % of the initial allocation, up to $max_allocation_usd
   (USD) per trader.

6. Identity and signature
   The Trader signs electronically as the person verified under case
   $kyc_case. The Firm records the signature with the text's SHA-256
   digest, the time, the signing login, network address and device.

7. Governing law
   $governing_law
